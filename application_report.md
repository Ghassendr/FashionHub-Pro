# Maison Tissue - Application Report

Maison Tissue is a centralized, premium e-commerce and logistics platform crafted specifically for the luxury fashion and haute couture industry. The application serves as a comprehensive ecosystem that connects four distinct user roles: Private Clients, Couture Houses, Fournisseurs (Fabric Suppliers), and Delivery Logistics Partners. At its core, the platform blends cutting-edge technology—such as AI-powered 3D body mapping and precision measurements—with intelligent logistics to ensure perfect couture fits and secure, white-glove delivery worldwide. By providing dedicated dashboards and specialized workflows for each role, Maison Tissue streamlines the entire bespoke fashion lifecycle from the initial fabric curation and custom tailoring to the final secure transportation of high-value garments.
